Disclaimer

The map shows public measurement data in a simpler way, without judgement or advice about buildings, the ground or their value. No rights can be derived from the map. Questions about a measurement go to the source. Comments about the site itself can be sent with the “Feedback” button on the map. This English version is a translation; in case of differences, the Dutch text prevails.

Zakkingskaart is a visualisation of existing public measurements, classified according to an existing standard (the KCAF guideline, table 3). No estimate per postcode, no judgement or advice and no foundation label. Independent initiative, separate from the municipality and Rijkswaterstaat; no rights can be derived from the data.
