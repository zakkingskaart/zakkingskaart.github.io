Settlement bolts are measuring points in façades whose height the city measures periodically. The recent rate is calculated from measurements at least 4 years apart. A settlement bolt says something about that one façade; the map and this page only show the measured rates. The median is the middle value: half sink faster, half slower.

Rijkswaterstaat benchmarks

There are no Rijkswaterstaat benchmarks in this neighbourhood.

Subsidence measured from space

European Union satellites measure the height of the ground. In this neighbourhood this covers 7 blocks of 100 by 100 metres. The typical (median) subsidence there is 0.9 mm per year (measured 2020–2025).

A satellite measures ground, streets and buildings together; for each block of 100 metres that is an average. A house on piles can stand still while the street in front of it sinks, and vice versa. Only where something reflects the radar signal well (roofs, roads) is there a measurement; not in meadows or on water.
